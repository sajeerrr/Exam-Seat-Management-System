SEATING VALIDATION CHECKS

40 seats in a room. That doesn't mean it's clash-free.

Equal headcounts can hide adjacent branch copying, room capacity overflows, and elective timing collisions. These four checks help you look beyond raw totals before seating plans are posted.

Branch Alternation Check

Can two students from the same department sit adjacent on the same bench? Never. ESMS automatically interleaves departments across every bench row to prevent copying during exams.

Dynamic Room Capacity Check

Does a classroom comfortably fit 45 or 30 exam seats? Adjust room capacities inline with stepper controls (+ / -) or rename halls on the fly without breaking seating integrity.

FN & AN Slot Pipeline Isolation

Chronological Forenoon (FN) first, Afternoon (AN) second slot ordering guarantees that morning and afternoon examinations remain completely isolated with dedicated blueprints.

University-Grade Clean CSV Export

Export audit-ready CSV spreadsheets with consecutive roll numbers compressed cleanly (e.g. 22CS01-22CS15), perfect for university portal uploads and physical noticeboards.
